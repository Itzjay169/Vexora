# Vexora Refund, Cancellation & Settlement Policy

> [!WARNING]
> **DRAFT ONLY - PENDING FORMAL LEGAL REVIEW BY QUALIFIED COUNSEL PRIOR TO PRODUCTION DEPLOYMENT.**  
> Applicable to tournament entries, organizer prize escrow, and internal platform credits.

## 1. Automated Tournament Cancellation
- **Under-Subscription**: If a tournament fails to meet its declared `min_participants` at registration cutoff time, the tournament is automatically cancelled by the system.
- **Immediate Full Refund**: All registered participants receive an instantaneous, automated release of their held credits (`RELEASE/REFUND` ledger transaction) with zero platform fee deduction.

## 2. Organiser-Initiated Cancellations
- An organiser may only request cancellation prior to match bracket generation.
- Player entry credits are immediately returned in full.
- Organisers who repeatedly cancel tournaments (>10% cancellation rate) will have their hosting privileges demoted or revoked, and their trust score penalized by 15 points.

## 3. Disconnection & Forfeit Rules
- Disconnections occurring during a live game match are governed by the specific publisher's competitive rules.
- If a player or team fails to check in or join the assigned lobby within 10 minutes of room code distribution, the match is recorded as a forfeit (`0-2` or last place) with no refund.

## 4. Dispute Settlements & Escrow Freezing
- In the event of a verified match dispute or organiser misconduct, Vexora moderators hold the authority to void match results and issue full credit restoration to affected players.
- Settlement payouts and organizer disbursements are strictly locked in Escrow during active disputes.
