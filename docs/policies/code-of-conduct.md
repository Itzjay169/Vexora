# Vexora Community Code of Conduct & Competitive Integrity Standards

> Applicable to all registered players, team captains, streamers, and tournament organisers on the Vexora Esports Platform.

---

## 1. Core Commitment to Sportsmanship
Vexora is dedicated to fostering a competitive, inclusive, and transparent esports environment. Every participant—whether playing in a grassroots community skirmish or a premier invitational tournament—is expected to uphold the highest standards of sportsmanship, respect, and fair competition.

---

## 2. Competitive Integrity & Fair Play
Every player and team must compete to the best of their skill and ability. The following actions are strictly prohibited:

1. **Cheating & Unauthorized Software**:
   - The use of aim-bots, trigger-bots, ESP/wall-hacks, memory manipulation tools, speed hacks, recoil scripts, or hardware macros.
   - Bypassing mobile device restrictions via unapproved emulators, rooted/jailbroken devices concealing integrity checks, or device spoofing tools.
2. **Exploiting & Bug Abuse**:
   - Intentionally exploiting known game bugs, glitching through map geometry, or manipulating publisher network vulnerabilities to gain an unnatural competitive advantage.
3. **Collusion & Match-Fixing**:
   - Conspiring with opposing players or teams to predetermine match results, artificially distribute kills, intentionally throw matches, or engage in "soft-play".
   - Splitting prize pools or trading tournament outcomes with opponents.
4. **Smurfing, Ringers & Multi-Accounting**:
   - Registering alternative accounts to compete in lower-tier brackets ("smurfing").
   - Using an unlisted player ("ringer") to play on a registered team member's account.
   - Every player must compete using their own verified Vexora account and their own linked in-game UID.

---

## 3. Communication & Behavioral Standards
Vexora maintains zero tolerance for abusive or toxic conduct across all platform surfaces (match lobby chat, team channels, support tickets, and associated tournament streams):

1. **Harassment & Hate Speech**:
   - Any harassment, threats of physical harm, hate speech, slurs, sexual harassment, or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, religion, caste, sexual orientation, gender identity, or disability is grounds for immediate Level 4 permanent termination.
2. **Doxxing & Personal Safety**:
   - Publishing, sharing, or threatening to share personal private data (PII) of any player, organiser, or administrator (including real names, physical locations, phone numbers, or private social profiles) is strictly prohibited.
3. **Gamer Impersonation**:
   - Falsely claiming to be a Vexora staff member, moderator, tournament referee, or professional player.

---

## 4. Enforcement & Sanctions
Violations of this Code of Conduct are categorized and processed through Vexora's Progressive Sanctions Framework (as detailed in ADR-011):
- **Level 1**: Formal Warning & Moderation Record.
- **Level 2**: Competitive Restriction (7-day tournament suspension).
- **Level 3**: Account Suspension (30-day complete access freeze).
- **Level 4**: Permanent Termination & Hardware/UID Blacklist.

Any user who witnesses a violation is encouraged to submit an in-game report accompanied by timestamped evidence via the Vexora Reporting Desk.
