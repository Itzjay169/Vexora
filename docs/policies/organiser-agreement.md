# Vexora Organiser Agreement & Operational Standards

> [!WARNING]
> **DRAFT ONLY - PENDING FORMAL LEGAL REVIEW BY QUALIFIED COUNSEL PRIOR TO PRODUCTION DEPLOYMENT.**  
> Binding terms for all tournament creators and community tournament organizers.

## 1. Organiser Duties & Fiduciary Responsibility
- **Punctuality**: Organisers must create match lobbies, generate room codes, and distribute credentials strictly according to the tournament schedule.
- **Fair Adjudication**: Organisers must review score screens objectively. Fabricating results, awarding unfair points, or favoring affiliate teams constitutes fraud and causes permanent ban and forfeiture of escrow funds.
- **Prize Escrow**: For tournaments featuring credit or cash prizes (when legally enabled), the organiser must pre-fund the complete prize pool into the Vexora Escrow Account prior to opening registrations.

## 2. Organiser Verification Tiers & Promotion
1. **Tier 1 - Starter**:
   - Host free tournaments up to 32 players / 8 teams.
   - Prohibited from charging entry credits or soliciting external fees.
2. **Tier 2 - Verified**:
   - Must provide legal identity documentation (Government ID / PAN / KYC).
   - Capable of hosting tournaments up to 128 players with entry fees (when legally unlocked).
   - Standard dispute SLA (24 hours).
3. **Tier 3 - Partner**:
   - Requires verified business entity, portfolio of 20+ successfully concluded tournaments with >95% completion rate.
   - High-capacity tournaments, sponsored partner banners, custom bracket workflows.

## 3. Room Code Security & Distribution Controls
- Room credentials must only be released to checked-in, verified participants via the Vexora in-app lobby screen.
- Distributing room codes on public Discord, Telegram, or WhatsApp channels is strictly prohibited to prevent unauthorized room crashing and spectator griefing.
