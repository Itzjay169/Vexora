# Vexora Acceptable Use & Anti-Fraud Policy

> [!WARNING]
> **DRAFT ONLY - PENDING FORMAL LEGAL REVIEW BY QUALIFIED COUNSEL PRIOR TO PRODUCTION DEPLOYMENT.**  
> Applicable to all digital services, APIs, applications, and networks operated by Vexora.

---

## 1. Overview & Purpose
This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) outlines the acceptable and prohibited uses of the Vexora network, APIs, web applications, and mobile clients. Compliance with this policy is mandatory for all account holders, third-party developers, and tournament organisers.

---

## 2. Infrastructure & System Security Prohibitions
Users must not engage in any activity that degrades, disrupts, or compromises the security, stability, or integrity of the Vexora platform. Strictly forbidden actions include:

1. **Reverse Engineering & Scraping**:
   - Decompiling, disassembling, reverse engineering, or deriving source code from Vexora mobile binaries or web bundles.
   - Scraping platform leaderboards, user profiles, tournament brackets, or market data using automated scripts, bots, spiders, or headless crawlers without written API authorization.
2. **Denial of Service & Abuse**:
   - Launching or facilitating Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, flood requests, or automated spamming against platform endpoints, tournament lobby servers, or real-time WebSocket gateways.
   - Circumventing or attempting to bypass platform rate limiters, web application firewalls (WAF), or IP reputation filters.
3. **Vulnerability Exploitation & Probing**:
   - Scanning, testing, or probing platform infrastructure for vulnerabilities without prior written consent from Vexora Security Operations under an authorized bug bounty agreement.
   - Injecting malicious scripts, SQL payloads, cross-site scripting (XSS) vectors, or binary exploits into form fields or evidence upload endpoints.

---

## 3. Financial & Credit Integrity
Vexora operates a closed-loop internal service credit ledger. Any attempts to manipulate financial balances or abuse promotional mechanics will result in immediate prosecution and regulatory referral:

1. **Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & Fraud**:
   - Using platform accounts or credit transfers to facilitate money laundering, tax evasion, terrorist financing, or illicit capital flight.
   - Initiating fraudulent chargebacks, disputing legitimate payment gateway settlements, or utilizing stolen credit cards/UPI handles.
2. **Secondary Market & Unauthorized Exchange**:
   - Trading, selling, or brokering Vexora platform credits, accounts, redeem codes, or tournament berths on external secondary markets (e.g., unauthorized Telegram groups, Discord servers, or third-party marketplaces) for physical currency or off-platform assets.
3. **Redeem Code Exploitation**:
   - Brute-forcing, fuzzing, or sharing unissued cryptographic redeem codes.
   - Reselling promotional voucher codes distributed for sponsored partner activations.

---

## 4. Evidence & Media Upload Guidelines
1. **Permissible Media**:
   - Evidence files uploaded to match disputes or profile verification must be genuine image (`JPEG`, `PNG`) or video (`MP4`) captures directly pertaining to match verification.
2. **Prohibited Content**:
   - Uploading files containing malware, viruses, ransomware, trojans, or corrupted data payloads.
   - Uploading sexually explicit content, depictions of gratuitous violence, hate symbols, or copyrighted materials owned by third parties without appropriate authorization.

---

## 5. Violation Consequences & Legal Recourse
Failure to comply with this AUP constitutes a material breach of the Terms of Service and will trigger:
- Immediate revocation of API access and account termination.
- Permanent forfeiture of pending tournament credits and escrowed funds.
- Referral to the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) and law enforcement authorities where criminal hacking, identity fraud, or money laundering is identified.
