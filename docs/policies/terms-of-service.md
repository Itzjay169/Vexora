## 1. Nature of the Platform & v1 Scope
Vexora is a skill-based esports tournament competition platform. Vexora does not offer games of chance, betting, wagering, or lottery mechanics. All outcomes are strictly determined by the competitive electronic gaming performance of registered players.

**v1 Public Launch Policy:**
The initial public release of Vexora operates **exclusively with FREE tournaments** (zero entry fees, zero monetary stakes, platform ranking and digital badge rewards only). Real-money tournaments and paid credit mechanics are disabled in software and will only be activated following written statutory legal sign-off.

## 2. Eligibility & Account Creation
- Players must be at least 16 years of age. Players aged 16-17 require verifiable parental consent and are restricted exclusively to free-to-play tournaments. Minors are barred from monetary prize competitions under all circumstances.
- Each individual is permitted exactly one Vexora account. Smurfing, multi-accounting, account sharing, or false identity documentation will result in immediate disqualification and account termination.
- Users must link their genuine in-game UID. Misrepresenting game identity or using someone else's in-game credentials is a Level 4 violation.

## 3. Fair Play & Anti-Cheat
- Cheating, scripting, macro usage, memory manipulation, wall-hacks, aim-bots, or exploiting in-game bugs is strictly forbidden.
- Collusion, soft-play, match-fixing, or intentional disconnects are considered fraud.
- Vexora reserves the right to demand video recordings, monitor device integrity signals, and submit suspicious match evidence to third-party publisher anti-cheat authorities.

## 4. Internal Platform Credits & Prizes
- Platform credits are closed-loop virtual units valid only on the Vexora network.
- Credits are not banknotes, bank deposits, or bearer instruments.
- Promotional credits are non-transferable and subject to published expiration dates.
- Payout requests for cash prizes (when legally enabled in future releases) are subject to KYC verification, anti-money laundering (AML) checks, applicable tax deductions at source (TDS under Section 194BA of the Indian Income Tax Act where applicable), and maker-checker administrative approvals.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Vexora is not liable for game publisher server outages, network latency, device failures, or third-party telecommunication disruptions occurring during live matches.
