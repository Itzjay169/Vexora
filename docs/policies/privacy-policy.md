# Vexora Privacy Policy & Data Retention Policy

> [!WARNING]
> **DRAFT ONLY - PENDING FORMAL LEGAL REVIEW BY QUALIFIED COUNSEL PRIOR TO PRODUCTION DEPLOYMENT.**  
> Aligned with Republic of India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, Information Technology Act 2000, and IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules 2021.

## 1. Principles of Data Processing
Vexora operates on a **Privacy-by-Design** and **Data Minimisation** philosophy:
- We collect only the data strictly necessary for competition integrity, identity confirmation, financial safety, and platform operations.
- We never sell personal data or broker user behavioral profiles.

## 2. Information We Collect
| Data Category | Specific Elements | Purpose | Retention Period |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Account Info** | Username, email, phone number | Identity & security alerts | Duration of account + 180 days (IT Rules 2021) |
| **Game Data** | In-game UID, In-Game Name (IGN) | Match bracket & result verification | Duration of account |
| **KYC / Verification** | Government ID, legal name, PAN | Organiser approval & legal payout compliance | 5 years (PMLA & IT Act mandate) |
| **Match Evidence** | Score screenshots, clip recordings, SHA-256 hashes | Dispute resolution & fraud prevention | 12 months after case closure |
| **Audit Logs** | Actor ID, IP hash, action timestamp | Tamper-evident system security | 5 years (CERT-In compliance) |

## 3. Data Principal Rights (DPDP Act 2023)
Users have enforceable statutory rights under the DPDP Act:
1. **Right to Access & Summary**: Request an export of all processed personal data in machine-readable JSON format.
2. **Right to Correction**: Update incorrect details via profile or support ticket.
3. **Right to Erasure**: Request account deletion. Data not required for active legal, financial, or anti-fraud compliance will be permanently purged within 30 days.
4. **Right to Nominate**: Nominate an individual to exercise data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

## 4. Protection of Minors (DPDP Act Section 9)
- Vexora does not engage in behavioral tracking, profiling, or targeted advertising directed at individuals under 18 years of age.
- Users aged 16-17 must provide verifiable parental/guardian consent.
- Minors are strictly prohibited from participating in any monetary or real-value tournaments.
