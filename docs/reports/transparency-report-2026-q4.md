## 1. Executive Summary & Launch Principle

Vexora is committed to being India's most transparent, fair, and trustworthy esports competition platform. 

In strict adherence to our core launch principle (Blueprint Section 6 & Section 28), **Vexora v1 is 100% FREE for all players and organisers**. No real-money entry fees or cash wagering are enabled. All internal balances, prize pools, and tournament tiers are operated under strict free-to-play skill competition guidelines. All real-money modules remain locked behind dual-approval security gates and state-by-state legal compliance certificates.

This inaugural Transparency Report discloses our operational metrics, dispute resolutions, fair-play enforcement, statutory compliance under the **Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021**, and the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023**.
